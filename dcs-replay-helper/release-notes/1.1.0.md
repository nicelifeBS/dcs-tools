The event list is easier to follow during playback.

**What's new**
- **Side colors:** events involving the blue or red side are listed in a muted blue or red. The colors get lighter on a dark Windows theme. Bookmarks take the side of the aircraft they were made on.
- **Past events scroll away:** an event is grayed out once its pre-roll can't be reached. 1 s after it happens, it scrolls smoothly out of the list, so the next events stay at the top.
- **Show past events:** a new checkbox next to the search box keeps past events listed, grayed, as before. It's remembered.

**Changed**
- To use **Sync to selected event** on an event that has already left the list, tick **Show past events** first.

**Upgrading**
- Download the new `DCSReplayHelper.exe` and replace the old one. The DCS hook hasn't changed, so you don't need to reinstall it.

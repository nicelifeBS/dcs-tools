Record one moment, then keep going without missing the next one. DCS no longer needs to be in front for the app to work.

**What's new**
- **Stop before bookmarks:** while the replay plays, it pauses the pre-roll before each bookmark of the Tacview recording, so you can change the camera and record on without running past the next moment you marked. The camera and the speed are left as they are. "Next stop" under **Seek** says where it'll pause. It's on by default; untick **Stop before bookmarks** to turn it off.
  - Seeks jump past bookmarks. After a jump, Play runs through the event you jumped to and stops before the next bookmark after it.
- **Show the event's aircraft (F2):** when you jump to an event from the list, DCS switches to the F2 view of the aircraft involved: the shooter, the aircraft lost or ejected from, or the one a bookmark names. The log says which, or why not. Untick the box to keep your own camera.
- **Speed changes without keystrokes:** the hook now changes time acceleration itself, so the DCS window no longer needs to be in front.
- **Better shooters and ejections:** launches and ejections name the nearest aircraft of the weapon's side, and F-4E seats count as ejections.

**Removed**
- **Post-roll** and **Play through**. Stop before bookmarks covers what they were for.

**Upgrading**
- Download the new `DCSReplayHelper.exe` and replace the old one.
- **Update the DCS hook:** choose **Tools → Install / update DCS hook…** and restart DCS. The hook is now 0.2.0. With the old hook the app still works, but it presses the speed keys as before and can't switch the F2 view; the log says so.

First release of **DCS Replay Helper**: plan your shots in Tacview, and let the app take a running DCS track replay there.

**What it does**
- **Seeks to a moment:** pick a Tacview bookmark, a launch, a kill or an ejection from the list, or type a replay or mission time.
- **Speed cap:** jumps forward at the speed you choose, 4x by default. The replay is never allowed to run faster.
- **Pre-roll and post-roll:** pauses a few seconds before the event, on the exact frame, then optionally plays through and pauses again just after it.
- **Time sync:** lines up Tacview time with the replay automatically, including the map's time zone. A fine adjustment and "sync to selected event" are there if needed, saved per recording.
- **Hook installer:** a menu item puts the hook into your Saved Games folder.

**Getting started**
1. Download `DCSReplayHelper.exe` and run it. There's nothing to install.
2. Choose **Tools → Install / update DCS hook…**, then restart DCS.
3. Play a track, open its Tacview recording (**File → Open Tacview recording…**), and double-click a bookmark.

**Notes**
- **Windows only.** Speed changes are sent as DCS's own time-acceleration keys, so keep the DCS window open (not minimised).
- **Unsigned exe.** Windows SmartScreen may warn on first start: choose *More info → Run anyway*.

Follow an aircraft with the free camera: pick it from a list and the camera's speed is set to match.

**What's new**
- **Camera speed tab:** lists the aircraft, helicopters and missiles within a distance of the camera (20 km by default) with type, pilot or unit name, group, speed (kt, km/h or m/s), the speed along the camera's view, and distance. Filter by kind, and the choices are remembered.
- **Match camera speed:** select an object and click the button (or double-click it). The app sets the free camera's (F11) speed to the object's speed along the way the camera looks, so the camera keeps up with it, flying forward, or backward if you look back at it.
  - It uses DCS's own keys and mouse wheel: `LAlt+Num*` and `LAlt+Num/` for the bulk, the wheel for the rest. It then reads the camera's speed from the hook and corrects it until it is within 1 m/s. That takes one or two rounds, a few seconds in all.
  - The DCS window comes to the front and the mouse pointer moves over it for a moment.
  - **Paused:** DCS ignores those keys and the wheel while paused. The replay runs at 1/16x while the speed is set, then pauses again at the speed it had.
  - **Key presses per round** is a setting (default 50). Lower it if DCS ever misses presses.
- **Camera speed readout** under the list: what the camera does now, forward or backward.

**Good to know**
- The camera only flies along the way it looks. Looking along an object's flight path gives its full speed; looking sideways gives little. The log says how much of the object's speed is across the view, which the camera can't follow.
- DCS doesn't report an object's speed, so the hook works it out from how far the object moved. Speeds show after the replay has played for a moment, and after a seek.
- Missiles are listed, but are too fast and short-lived for the camera to be worth matching. Helicopters and missiles have not been tried in a real replay yet.
- The pilot column shows the unit name from the mission, with "(player)" for human players.

**Upgrading**
- Download the new `DCSReplayHelper.exe` and replace the old one.
- **Update the DCS hook:** choose **Tools → Install / update DCS hook…** and restart DCS. The hook is now 0.3.0. With the old hook everything else still works, and the Camera speed tab says the hook is too old.
